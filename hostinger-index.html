About

Scientist. Engineer. Problem-solver.

I started as a scientist at Rutgers. Lab work, validated protocols, nutritional and mineral analysis—the kind of work where precision is not optional. That precision is what brought me into AI training early, where I was promoted to reviewing and correcting the work of other researchers and engineers.

That same instinct took me to Columbia Engineering to study machine learning, neural networks, and transformers from the inside. Now I bring it to small and mid-sized businesses: finding what is broken, understanding why, and helping make it work better.
